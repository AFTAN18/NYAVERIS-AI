# LinkedIn project explanation — draft

We built **NYAVERIS AI** for PromptWars 2026 at SRMIST, aligned with UN SDG 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth.

The idea: workers need more than a record of attendance. They need a clear way to understand a pay difference, raise a safety concern and see what happened next.

Our **Fairness Twin + ProofLoop Engine** connects confirmed work to employer payroll and a documented resolution workflow. In the construction demo, ten payable hours produce ₹1,100 under the organization's configured example policy, while payroll records ₹950. The worker can request a review of the ₹150 difference, the employer can correct or explain it, and the worker confirms the outcome. **Fairness Replay** shows the history.

**SafeSignal** supports anonymous concerns with a random reference and separate secret tracking credential. **RestGuard** detects configured scheduling risks and lets a hospital administrator test how a later shift changes a six-hour rest window to eleven hours before confirming a schedule change.

One platform supports construction and healthcare, with worker and employer interfaces, English/Tamil/Hindi core flows, Supabase Auth/PostgreSQL RLS and deterministic wage calculations. Saarthi provides multilingual explanations with optional Gemini and a text fallback; AI never decides wages or resolves safety complaints.

The goal is practical accountability: clearer pay, safer schedules and a stronger worker voice. Demonstration policies are not legal advice, and anonymous reporting does not promise absolute anonymity.
